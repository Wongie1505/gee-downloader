About GEE Downloader

Google Earth Engine holds decades of satellite data, but getting it out means writing JavaScript and clicking Run for every export. This app does that part for you. Choose what you need, and the files land in your own Google Drive.

How it works

  1. Log in with your Google account and your Earth Engine project.
  2. Pick a dataset, then an area: a district, an uploaded file, or one you draw.
  3. Choose the years and how to group them.
  4. Download. Earth Engine runs the job and saves the files in Drive.

What you need

A Google account registered for Earth Engine, with a Cloud project ID. Registration is free for non-commercial use. While the app is in testing, only Google accounts added by the developer can log in.

Privacy

This site has no server. Login happens with Google, and your exports go to your own Drive. Nothing about you is stored here.

Log in

Sign in with Google and tell us which Earth Engine project to use. Your exports run on your own project and land in your own Drive.

Not sure?

Google opens in a pop-up. Allow pop-ups if your browser blocks it.

Get access in 5 minutes

  1. Register a project. Open the Earth Engine registration page, sign in, and create a project. Choose the unpaid, noncommercial option.
  2. Finish the questionnaire. Google checks that your use is noncommercial. Access starts when it is done.
  3. Copy your Project ID. In the Cloud console the ID shows in the project picker, next to the project name. It is lowercase with dashes.
  4. Get on the tester list. While this app is in testing, ask the developer to add your Google account.
